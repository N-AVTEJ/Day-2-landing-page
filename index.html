Frequently Asked

Answers to common questions.

Everything you need to know about NEXORA, workspace configuration, and team collaboration.

What is NEXORA?

+

NEXORA is a modern AI-powered productivity platform created to help startup founders, developers, and remote teams organize projects, automate routine chores, and collaborate without context switching.

Is there a free plan?

+

Yes. The Starter plan is 100% free forever and includes up to 3 active project boards, unlimited tasks, and 7 days of activity history. You can begin organizing your workflow immediately without entering credit card details.

Can I collaborate with my team?

+

Absolutely. NEXORA supports collaborative workspaces with live multi-user updates, assignee tagging, contextual discussions, and role-based permissions designed to keep everyone in sync.

Can I cancel a subscription at any time?

+

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription at any point directly from your workspace billing dashboard with zero penalty fees or lock-in commitments.
